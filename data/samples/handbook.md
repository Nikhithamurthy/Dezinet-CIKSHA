# Dezinet Employee Handbook

This handbook explains how work is organised at Dezinet. It is updated every quarter by the people team. Read it during your first week and keep it handy.

## Leave Policy

Every full-time employee receives 20 days of paid leave per year. Leave accrues monthly and up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Request leave at least 7 days in advance through the people portal. Sick leave does not count against the 20 days, but a medical note is required after 3 consecutive days.

## Support Hours

The customer support team is available Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM IST. Urgent production incidents are handled by the on-call engineer at any hour. Customers can raise tickets through the help center, and every ticket receives a first response within 4 working hours. Escalations go to the support lead.

## Pricing

Dezinet offers three subscription plans for teams.

| Plan | Price | Seats |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $10 per user | 5 |
| Team | $25 per user | 50 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited |

Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount on every plan. Taxes are added at checkout based on the billing country.

## Security

All employees must use a password manager and hardware-backed two factor authentication. Customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Never paste customer data into public tools. Report any suspected incident to the security team immediately so the response process can start within the hour.
